## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Tummy Scanner ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

## No Data Collection

Tummy Scanner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or user information. Specifically:

- We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data.
- We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or similar tracking technologies.
- We do not create user profiles or accounts.

## Microphone Access

The App may request access to your device's microphone solely for in-app entertainment features. Audio captured through the microphone is processed entirely on your device and is **never recorded, stored, or transmitted** to any server or third party.

## No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any user data to third parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing to share.

## No Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data collection services.

## Entertainment Purpose Only

Tummy Scanner is designed solely for entertainment purposes. The App:

- Is **not** a medical device and should **not** be used for medical diagnosis, treatment, or health monitoring.
- Does **not** provide medical advice of any kind.
- Should **not** be used as a substitute for professional medical consultation.
- Makes no claims regarding the accuracy or reliability of any information presented within the App.

If you have health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations by virtue of collecting no data whatsoever.

## Data Security

As the App does not collect or store any user data, there is no personal data at risk. All app interactions occur locally on your device.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no user data to access, modify, or delete. You can remove the App from your device at any time, which will remove all locally stored app data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
