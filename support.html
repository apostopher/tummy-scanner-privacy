Frequently Asked Questions

What does Tummy Scanner do?

Tummy Scanner is a fun, interactive app that lets kids explore the human digestive system. Swipe to scan and tap on organs to learn about them!

Does the app collect my data?

No. Tummy Scanner does not collect, store, or share any personal data whatsoever. Everything stays on your device.

Is this a real medical scanner?

No! Tummy Scanner is purely for fun and learning. It is not a medical device and should never be used in place of professional medical advice.

Does it work on iPad?

Tummy Scanner is currently designed for iPhone only.

Why does the app ask for microphone access?

The microphone is used for fun in-app sound features. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
